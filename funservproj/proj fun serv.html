Our History

Solara Farewell was started by a close group of friends who share a deep respect for honoring the lives of those who have passed. Guided by their belief in celebrating a life well lived, they set out to create a funeral service company that offers more care and warmth than the usual approach.

Each founder brings different experiences, but they all share the same goal: to give families a compassionate and personal way to say goodbye. They wanted to offer services that not only handle the practical needs but also provide a comforting and dignified experience that reflects each person’s unique story.

At Solara Farewell, families can expect thoughtful support and a service that truly honors their loved one.

Grief Support

At Solara Farewell we want families to feel supported even after the funeral has ended. We recognize that grief is a gradual process and that each person experiences grief differently. Because of this we provide grief support and a range of resources to assist you during the challenging period that follows a loss.

Our support services are intended to offer comfort and clear guidance whenever you need it. We can connect you with professional counselors, established support groups and reliable information that may help you better understand and manage your emotions.
